Data Deletion & Account Deletion for JLIO

App Name: JLIO – Q&A, Stories & Chat (com.jlio.app)

Developer: Z's Dev

1. Account Deletion (Complete Account Removal)

If you have created an account in JLIO, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the app:

  • Open the JLIO app
  • Tap Profile (bottom right)
  • Tap Settings (gear icon)
  • Select Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion (this action cannot be undone)
⚠️ Important: Deleting your account will permanently remove:
  • Your account and all associated profile data
  • All messages and conversations
  • All stories and posts
  • All pairing data
  • All saved preferences and settings
This action is permanent and cannot be reversed.

2. Data Deletion (Partial Data Removal)

If you prefer to keep your account but want to delete specific data, you can delete your data directly inside the JLIO app:

  • Open the JLIO app
  • Tap Profile (bottom right)
  • Tap Settings (gear icon)
  • Select Privacy → Delete Data

This will remove:

  • Anonymous messages
  • Stories and posts
  • Pairing data and chat history

Note: Some non-identifiable analytics data may be retained for legal or security purposes.

3. If You Cannot Access the App

If you are unable to access the JLIO app or experience technical difficulties requesting account deletion, please contact our support team:

Email: support@jlio.app

Legal Inquiries: legal@jlio.app

Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line and provide your account information. We will process your request within 30 days.

4. Data Processing Timeline

Once you request account deletion:

  • Your account is immediately deactivated and inaccessible
  • All data is marked for deletion and removed from our systems within 30 days
  • You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

5. What Data We Retain

In compliance with legal and security requirements, we may retain:

  • Non-identifiable, aggregated analytics data
  • Data necessary for legal compliance or fraud prevention
  • Backup copies for a limited period (deleted within 90 days)
Questions? For more information about our privacy practices and data handling, please visit our Safety & Privacy Policy or contact support at support@jlio.app.
